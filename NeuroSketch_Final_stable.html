About NeuroSketch v3

Tracking: MediaPipe FaceMesh 468+10 iris landmarks

Algorithm: 7-feature regression (iris XY + nose XY + head yaw + pitch + bias). Kalman filter smoothing. Target assist snap.

Calibration: 25-point 5×5 grid. Leave-one-out cross-validation accuracy estimate.

All processing on-device. No camera data sent anywhere. Free and open source.
