The Solution
NeuroGaze — Open Source
IR Eye Tracker at $149
👁
IR Camera
Raspberry Pi NoIR camera. No IR filter. Sees the corneal reflection from our LED ring.
💡
850nm LED Ring
8 near-infrared LEDs around the lens. Illuminates the eye, creates the precise corneal reflection reference point.
🧠
P-CR Tracking
Pupil-Corneal Reflection vector. Head-movement independent. Same math as every $15,000 device.
💻
NeuroSketch App
Free, offline, browser-based AAC. Communication board, drawing, keyboard, rehab, emergency SOS.
